# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect private information and security-sensitive camera data.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and review decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate
misinformation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption
are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially and
fairly as practical.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, issue trackers, pull requests,
release channels, and public representation of the project.
